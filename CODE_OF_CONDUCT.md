# Code of Conduct

OpenExam contributors are expected to keep project spaces focused, respectful, and useful.

## Expected Behavior

- Be direct, constructive, and professional.
- Discuss technical decisions with evidence and clear tradeoffs.
- Respect different backgrounds, experience levels, and communication styles.
- Keep criticism focused on code, documentation, product decisions, and project outcomes.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Repeatedly derailing issues or pull requests.
- Sharing secrets, private user data, or copyrighted material without authorization.

## Reporting

If a private security or conduct channel has not been published yet, avoid posting sensitive details publicly. Contact the maintainers through the safest available project channel and include enough context for review without exposing secrets or private data.

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, reject contributions, or restrict participation when behavior harms the project or its contributors.
